# Sailing, boss records and wealth

This is an authored OSRS-inspired simulation, not an attempt to report real accounts or exact drop tables. Item valuations use a shared daily OSRS Wiki GE snapshot. Sailing joins the shared skill list as skill 24. It has a provisional ordinary training ceiling of 95,000 XP/hour before the world multiplier; the existing Wise Old Man capture predates Sailing. Max total level is 2,376. Combat level keeps its existing formula.

## Player state and upgrade

`normalizeUser` adds missing Sailing at zero XP / level 1. Existing skill XP, account timestamps and unlock history are retained. Historical max-account or 2,277 milestones are not deleted. New 2,376, Sailing mastery and Sailing 200M milestones can be earned.

Each player receives persistent aptitude, risk tolerance, thrift, dedication and a favorite boss. Opening traits use a username seed; after publication they are saved with the account and survive renames. The opening bank estimate is proportional to existing total XP, multiplied by a uniform variation and a lognormal factor. It is bounded between 5,000 GP and 2B GP. This is labeled as an estimate. No historical kills, rare drops or earned-income history are fabricated. Existing saved bank values, counters and traits take precedence on every rebuild. The move to ordinary-time economics preserves previous balances and KC; earlier multiplier-driven gains cannot be reliably reconstructed and are not retrospectively removed.

The bank counter represents modeled cash, banked materials and equipment. On the first scheduled update with the new model, 30% of an existing balance becomes modeled cash and 70% becomes an equal-value basket of abyssal whips and amulets of fury at that update's prices. This preserves the opening value exactly; the fractional quantities are a representative equipment basket, not an assertion that an account owns those exact items. Opening wealth is estimated once, never repeatedly topped up as accelerated XP rises.

Income adds cash or retained item quantities. Spending uses cash first and then liquidates the same proportion of every retained item. The bank reconciles as opening estimate + earned income − actual spending + market revaluation, subject to rounding and a 1T GP safety cap. Only the latest economic session and aggregate income, spending, rare drops, deaths and revaluation are kept. Item holdings have a fixed catalog bound; there is no growing transaction log.

## Daily GE pricing

The existing 15-minute scheduled job calls `dailyMarket` before progressing players. At most one bulk request is attempted per UTC day to `https://prices.runescape.wiki/api/v1/osrs/latest`, with a descriptive User-Agent and an eight-second timeout. Only catalog items used by the model are retained in one `system:ge-prices` document. No profile read or per-player session makes external requests. Snapshot writes and player publication share the existing Durable Object transaction, so a failed world update rolls both back.

Prices use the midpoint of valid high/low quotes (or a single valid side), retaining each item's observation timestamp. Quotes must be positive finite numbers observed within seven days. An incomplete, invalid or failed response preserves the last complete snapshot. With no successful snapshot, bundled OSRS Wiki midpoint prices captured on 2026-10-26 UTC are used. Failure is recorded so the system does not retry every tick; the next UTC day attempts another refresh. Profiles show the capture date, current/stale/fallback status and cumulative market revaluation. This is a daily snapshot of recent trades, not a daily average or a continuously live feed.

At each scheduled update, even inactive accounts have retained quantities revalued once at the shared prices. Earnings and spending counters do not change when prices move. Common boss loot and skilling materials are representative item baskets calibrated to the original authored GP rates at the bundled baseline. Supplies track a shark/prayer-potion price index. These are approximations to material mixes and supply usage, not exact inventories or every OSRS drop table. Rare rewards use priced representative items, including weighted mixes of raid rewards so both modest rewards and jackpots remain possible. Rare prices come directly from the snapshot; no extra random GP multiplier is applied to an individual rare item. Scurrius and Mole receive no invented tradeable rare reward.

## Coupled sessions

A sampled activity duration is divided between bossing and ordinary skill training. They share the same elapsed-time budget. Inactive sessions do neither. Boss selection depends on archetype, a shared daily appetite for that player's preferred boss, personal dedication, familiarity and appetite for harder encounters. Combat/skill gates and a minimum supply budget limit eligibility. Quest completion, team composition and exact equipment inventories are not separately simulated; the gates are proxies for readiness.

Bossing consumes 60–100% of an active session when chosen. Its effective hours are ordinary boss hours, with no XP/world multiplier. Kill intensity is authored ordinary kills/hour multiplied by boss hours, personal aptitude, gear quality (a saturating logarithm of bank value), familiarity (saturating exponential of past KC), level readiness, and bounded lognormal session variation. Small kill counts use an exact Poisson draw; larger counts use a normal approximation. Each session has a 1,000-kill safety bound.

Kills award combat XP from the encounter's modeled HP, with related Hitpoints XP and Slayer XP where applicable. Only the resulting XP is multiplied by the period-weighted world XP scale, once. KC, drops, deaths and wealth never receive that scale. The unused session time trains a skill using the existing ordinary-rate model and the same world scale. Buyable methods lose efficiency when the player cannot afford full supplies, representing slower self-supplied alternatives.

Ordinary loot quantities use bounded lognormal session variation. Rare drops use geometric waiting gaps, equivalent to independent Bernoulli chances per kill without a loop over every kill. Supply costs depend on ordinary kills, GE supply prices and thrift; death frequency depends on ordinary boss hours, risk and familiarity, with wealth-dependent recovery costs. Spending never makes the bank negative. Skill income rises with level and varies by session; construction, prayer and other buyables consume wealth. Income, costs and buyable-skill affordability all use ordinary training time, regardless of the XP multiplier. Accelerated leveling can unlock harder content sooner, but never directly grants extra kills or GP.

The 16 encounter categories cover early bosses, Barrows, Dagannoth Kings, Zulrah, Vorkath, Muspah, four God Wars generals, Hydra, and three raids. Rates, material mixes, drop probabilities and gates are deliberately authored estimates, with GE pricing for the modeled tradeable quantities. A rare drop is a modeled valuable reward, not a claim about a real account. Raids count completions, Barrows counts chests, and Dagannoth Kings is an aggregate category.

## Rankings and storage budget

`/api/activity-rankings?category=bank|bosses|<boss-key>` provides the same pagination/name filtering as skill ladders. Zero-KC accounts are unranked; ties use player names, and filtering preserves global rank. Profiles carry bank/boss ranks from the same published generation as their state. New UI routes are `#wealth` and `#bosses?boss=vorkath`.

All computation happens inside the existing serialized world update. Rankings share the existing 500-player packed documents. Profiles use 125-player documents, so a cold batch across the whole 5,000-player world reads at most 40 profile documents plus the manifest. Existing generations with 100-player packs remain readable through their manifest indexes. The next successful update repacks them atomically. Public reads never initialize or write state. New ranking endpoints use the existing edge cache.

`npm run capacity` uses disposable local workerd/SQLite, creates 5,000 maxed accounts with all boss records populated, measures a scheduled update including index/deletion costs, and exercises cold batch/wealth/boss/profile reads. It rejects scheduled writes of 30,000/day or more, a conservative read projection of 4.5M/day or more at the 100,000 DO request limit (including scheduled calls), any public-read writes, documents of 1.9 MB or more, or a live dataset of 100 MB or more. These are local workload measurements, not production traffic/account-usage telemetry. Cloudflare allowances are shared with unrelated applications; administrative rebuilds and other account usage also need headroom.
